stain normalization, tissue segmentation, and feature analysis for downstream
machine learning. Grew out of my work in the Computational Microscopy Imaging Lab
on generative modeling {% cite lutnick2020generative %} and efficient training
pipelines {% cite lutnick2022histofetch %}.
